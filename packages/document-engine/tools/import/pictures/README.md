# Pictures of the occupational safety set

Sources of the pictures `../build_pictures.py` writes into 2.2, 9 and 11. `make_pictures.py` renders
`svg/` into `png/` (PyMuPDF and Pillow); the PNGs are committed, so building the templates needs neither.

## Safety signs

The standard ISO 7010 pictograms as vector drawings from Wikimedia Commons, downloaded on 20 November 2026.
None needs attribution: every file is in the public domain or under CC0 by its Commons page. Most were
redrawn by a Commons contributor and released with `{{PD-self}}`; `{{PD-ineligible}}` marks a shape too simple
for copyright. ISO's standard itself is copyrighted; the registered signs are meant to be reproduced.

## Illustrations

`png/illustration-cpr.png`, `png/illustration-extinguisher.png`, `png/illustration-barrier.png` and `png/tape.png`
were generated for SSM Ușor with ChatGPT from one shared style prompt, then reduced to their print size at about
200 dpi (the tape icon at 600 dpi, beside the signs) and 128 colours.

## Drawn here

`make_pictures.py` draws the hazard bands of 2.2's Fig. 1 (`stripes-*.svg`) and 9's method figures 1.1 to 1.5 b
(`figure-*.svg`) from their geometry.
