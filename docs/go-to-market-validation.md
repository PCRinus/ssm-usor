# SSM Ușor — Pre-launch Go-to-Market Validation Plan

Status: working plan  
Last updated: 31 August 2026  
Primary audience: founders and early growth/product team

## 1. Recommendation

Build the landing page before the full product, but do not optimize it primarily for a large email waitlist.

The purpose of the first site is to validate four things:

1. external SSM providers recognize the fragmented workflow as an important problem;
2. the proposed document-to-signature workflow is materially better than their current process;
3. the right person is willing to discuss real workflows and migrate one real client;
4. providers will commit money or equivalent contractual effort to a pilot.

An email address proves curiosity. A qualified call, real sample workflow, and paid pilot provide progressively stronger evidence.

Primary conversion:

> Apply to become a design partner / book a 20-minute workflow review.

Secondary conversion:

> Join the launch list.

## 2. Who the landing page is for

The page should explicitly say it is built for:

- authorized external SSM providers / SEPP;
- provider owners and operations managers;
- SSM/SU specialists managing several client companies;
- small and larger provider teams with mixed-industry client portfolios.

Do not center the initial campaign on generic Romanian business owners. Employers are participants and downstream beneficiaries, but they are not the chosen buyer. Broad business-owner traffic will distort the message, lead quality, and product priorities.

### Optional separate employer-demand experiment

Business-owner advertising may later be useful as a second, clearly separated funnel:

> Ai nevoie de un serviciu extern SSM? Primește o discuție sau ofertă de la un furnizor verificat.

Qualified employer requests could be routed to design-partner providers. If it works, SSM Ușor would help providers win clients as well as operate them, making the software more valuable and reducing customer-acquisition friction.

Do not mix these visitors into the software waitlist or report them as provider demand. This is a lead-generation/marketplace hypothesis with different consent, routing, commercial, quality, and conflict-of-interest requirements. Test it only after the provider workflow proposition is validated.

## 3. Landing-page proposition

### Recommended hero

**Headline:**

> Toți clienții tăi SSM. Un singur loc de lucru.

**Subheadline:**

> Creezi și editezi documente, colectezi date de la clienți, organizezi instruiri și testări, semnezi electronic și urmărești toate termenele fără să muți fișiere între Word, PDF, email și alte aplicații.

**Primary CTA:** `Devino partener pilot`  
**Secondary CTA:** `Vreau să aflu când se lansează`

Alternative headline to test:

> De la document Word la fișă semnată, fără să părăsești platforma.

### Page structure

1. Hero with provider-specific promise and CTA.
2. “Cum lucrezi astăzi” flow: Excel/Word → PDF → email → signing tool → folders → manual reminders.
3. “Cum vei lucra în SSM Ușor” flow: client data → edit → review → sign → evidence, all connected.
4. Four product pillars: client portfolio, document studio, training/signature, obligations/evidence.
5. A short interactive prototype or 60–90 second product-concept video.
6. Concrete outcome examples: prepare a client document pack, process a new employee, run periodic instruction, close a corrective measure.
7. Design-partner offer and expectations.
8. Trust section: specialist review, appropriate signature provider, customer-controlled exports, privacy/security approach.
9. FAQ explaining what exists today, what is a prototype, and the expected pilot timing.
10. Final CTA and contact details.

Do not use fake customer logos, fabricated usage statistics, or screenshots that imply unbuilt features are already live. Label prototypes and planned capabilities clearly.

## 4. Qualification and forms

### Low-friction launch form

- work email;
- first and last name;
- provider/company name;
- role;
- explicit consent for product-launch communications.

### Design-partner qualification

Collect progressively, after the first CTA:

- number of active client companies;
- approximate managed employees;
- provider team size;
- SSM-only or SSM + SU/PSI services;
- tools used today: Word, Excel, Google Drive/OneDrive, current SSM platform, signing provider, other;
- most time-consuming recurring workflow;
- willingness to show anonymized real templates/processes;
- willingness to pilot with one client;
- desired contact method and calendar slot.

Do not require a document upload on the public landing form. Request anonymized samples through a secure, clearly explained flow after qualification.

Use separate consent choices for launch marketing and optional research participation. Someone requesting a pilot contact does not need to consent to unrelated marketing to receive the requested response.

## 5. Channel strategy

### Priority 1 — Direct design-partner recruitment

Use official county ITM lists to identify external-provider companies, then research each business through its own website, professional profiles, associations, referrals, and events.

The initial goal is 50–100 named target accounts and 12 completed interviews, not a mass mailing list.

Recommended approach:

- personalized founder outreach referencing the provider's actual business;
- a short request to understand its document workflow, not a generic product blast;
- introductions through SSM specialists, trust-service providers, occupational-medicine partners, training organizations, and professional associations;
- small roundtables or a webinar about reducing document administration for external services;
- offer a workflow audit and early influence over the product.

Publicly listed contact data is useful for identifying businesses, but it is not automatically permission for bulk marketing or for uploading those contacts to advertising platforms. Obtain Romanian privacy/ePrivacy counsel before scaled cold-email campaigns.

### Priority 2 — Google Search

Search is the best initial paid channel because it captures intent without needing Google to know the person's profession.

Start with exact and phrase match in Romanian. Candidate groups to validate in Keyword Planner and the live search-term report:

**Provider-workflow intent**

- `soft servicii externe ssm`
- `aplicație firmă ssm`
- `program administrare clienți ssm`
- `software protecția muncii clienți`
- `platformă sepp`

**Document and process pain**

- `generare documente ssm`
- `soft documente ssm`
- `editare documente ssm`
- `fișe ssm electronice`
- `semnare electronică fișe ssm`
- `instruire ssm online platformă`

**Category intent**

- `aplicație ssm`
- `software ssm`
- `platformă ssm`
- `digitalizare ssm`

Use negatives aggressively after reviewing actual queries, especially informational, employment, training-certificate, and free-template intent such as `job`, `angajare`, `salariu`, `curs`, `atestat`, `model gratuit`, and `pdf gratuit` when they do not convert.

Do not begin with broad Display, Demand Gen, or Performance Max campaigns. There will be too little conversion data and too much irrelevant business-owner traffic.

### Priority 3 — Partnerships and trusted distribution

- professional SSM associations and communities;
- SSM qualification/training organizations;
- electronic-signature and archival providers;
- occupational-medicine clinics and HR/payroll providers serving the same external services;
- SSM conferences, regional meetups, webinars, and specialist publications;
- referral incentive for design partners after product value is proven.

For a trust-sensitive vertical, a respected specialist introduction may outperform paid impressions.

### Priority 4 — LinkedIn organic and account-based outreach

LinkedIn supports job-title, company, industry, company-size, skill, and related targeting attributes. In practice, Romanian external SSM providers may use inconsistent titles and have very small teams, so paid audience size may be limited.

Use LinkedIn first for:

- founder connection and personalized messages;
- educational posts showing the broken workflow and prototype;
- searching titles such as `administrator`, `fondator`, `consultant SSM`, `specialist SSM`, `inspector SSM`, `coordonator SSM`, `conducător serviciu extern`, and related variants;
- account-based lists of known provider companies;
- event/webinar invitations.

Test paid LinkedIn only after measuring the size and forecasted cost of the Romanian audience inside Campaign Manager.

### Priority 5 — Meta advertising

Do not rely on cold Meta interest/job targeting to identify this niche reliably.

Use Meta initially for:

- retargeting consenting landing-page visitors;
- promoting educational video or webinar content;
- later, testing first-party or similar audiences only after there is a sufficiently large, lawfully collected audience and current platform rules permit it.

Broad “business owner” targeting is likely to generate inexpensive but low-quality registrations. If tested, isolate it in a small experiment and qualify respondents immediately.

### Priority 6 — Content and organic search

Create content for provider operations rather than general employer compliance:

- “Cum gestionezi 50 de clienți SSM fără 50 de foldere și calendare”;
- “Fluxul complet de la Word la fișă SSM semnată electronic”;
- “Cum urmărești documentele și instruirile restante la toți clienții”;
- “Checklist pentru migrarea unui serviciu extern SSM la documente digitale”;
- neutral comparisons of document workflows and signing options;
- practical legislative-change explanations with primary-source citations.

This compounds slowly but creates credibility and high-intent search inventory.

## 6. Initial campaign sequence

### Stage A — Message validation before ad spend

1. Build the landing page and two hero variants.
2. Create a clickable document-workflow prototype.
3. Recruit 12 external providers directly.
4. Observe at least 8 real, anonymized workflows.
5. Rewrite the page using their own language and objections.

### Stage B — Small Google Search test

- Romania only; Romanian language first.
- Separate provider-specific, document-pain, and broad category ad groups.
- Exact/phrase match first.
- Send each group to the most relevant landing-page section or variant.
- Optimize for qualified design-partner application or booked call, not page views.
- Review search terms frequently and add negatives.

An initial learning budget of roughly €750–€1,500 over four to six weeks is sufficient to test whether relevant search volume and qualified intent exist. The purpose is learning, not scaling.

### Stage C — Retargeting and educational event

- Retarget consenting visitors with a document-workflow demo or provider webinar.
- Invite directly recruited providers and partner audiences.
- Measure registrations, attendance, qualified conversations, and pilot interest.

### Stage D — Scale only after a sales signal

Increase spend only after at least three external providers commit to a pilot and the team understands which keyword/message generated them.

## 7. Metrics and decision gates

### Funnel events

1. relevant landing-page visit;
2. launch signup;
3. qualified provider form;
4. booked workflow call;
5. attended call;
6. real workflow/sample supplied;
7. pilot proposed;
8. paid/contracted pilot;
9. real client activated;
10. retained after two recurring cycles.

The primary acquisition metric is **cost per qualified external provider**, not cost per email signup.

### Recommended early gates

- At least 5% of high-intent Search visitors become qualified provider leads or book calls.
- At least half of booked calls attend.
- At least one-third of qualified conversations progress to a real-workflow review.
- At least three providers commit to a paid or contractually meaningful pilot.
- At least five providers independently rank Word/PDF/signature/email fragmentation among their top three operational problems.
- At least two pilot providers complete two recurring cycles and continue using the system.

Treat these as initial thresholds to learn from, not universal SaaS benchmarks. A small niche can produce volatile percentages.

### Stop or revise when

- paid traffic consists mostly of employers, job seekers, course seekers, or free-template seekers;
- providers register but will not take a call or show a workflow;
- interviews reveal that an existing platform already solves the proposed workflow adequately;
- migration and document fidelity cost more than the recoverable customer value;
- providers like the idea but will not commit money, time, or one real client.

## 8. Tracking, consent, and privacy

- Provide a clear privacy notice and identify the legal entity collecting leads.
- Use an appropriate consent-management platform before activating non-essential advertising/analytics tags in the EEA.
- Keep product-update consent separate from a requested sales response.
- Record the source and version of consent.
- Provide unsubscribe and deletion mechanisms.
- Do not upload scraped/public provider contacts to Google Customer Match or Meta Custom Audiences without a verified lawful basis and the permissions required by each platform.
- Google requires EEA consent signals for applicable Customer Match advertising uses; Meta's terms require necessary rights, permissions, and a lawful basis for customer-list audiences.
- Minimize form data and do not collect employee/client personal data during public lead generation.

This section requires Romanian legal review before campaigns launch.

## 9. First deliverables

1. Romanian landing-page copy and visual wireframe.
2. Two hero-message variants.
3. Design-partner qualification form and calendar flow.
4. Clickable prototype of template → client edit → PDF → signature → evidence.
5. Provider account list from current ITM sources, deduplicated and researched.
6. Interview script and workflow-observation template.
7. Google Search campaign draft with keyword groups and negatives.
8. Analytics event dictionary and consent implementation.
9. Four-week experiment dashboard.
