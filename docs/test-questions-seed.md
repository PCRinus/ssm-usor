# Built-in test questions, for review

The questions the migration `supabase/migrations/20261020100100_built_in_test_questions.sql`
seeds as built-in questions of the question bank ([ADR 024](architecture/adr-024-knowledge-tests.md)),
in the order the printed tests list them. Every organization reads them, hides the ones it does
not want and writes its own beside them; none edits them. A correction is a new migration that
updates a row in place under its fixed id.

Each question has two to four options, one or more of them correct (in bold, marked _corect_). A
sitting in the portal draws ten of a test's active questions and shuffles their options; a
question counts only when the options chosen are exactly the correct ones. The explanation is
what the study card shows before the test and what a failed attempt shows after a wrong answer.

Citations name the act the general training material (MISSMIG, 2.2) or the fire-safety own
instructions (IPSU, 2.1) quote for the answer. Where the answer is a rule of the own instructions
themselves and no act is quoted, the citation names the document and its article as the
engine numbers it: I.P.S.S.M. (3.2, common part) or IPSU.

The column _Change_ says what departs from the sample tests of the pack (5.1, fire 4.1 and
fire 4.2 before ADR 024). An empty cell means the question, its options and its key are the
sample's, with the option letters and the final semicolons dropped and the first letter
capitalized.
