# ADR 032: Employees imported from their training sheets

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-31

## Context

[ADR 030](adr-030-import-from-documents.md) decided two imports and built the first. The **employee import** (_import din fișe_) had one section there: photographs of the individual training sheets read one by one, pages grouped into people, job titles matched to positions, existing people completed, leavers unticked, rows turned into training records, the photographs kept as the employees' files. That was enough to place it after the client import, not to build it. The packs carry no staff list; the people, their CNPs, their hire dates and the whole history of their trainings are on the sheets, one or two pairs of pages per person, filled by hand over years and photographed with a phone when the provider changes. A client import without its employee import gives a provider its clients with nobody in them.

The screens were settled on the design canvas "Import clienți din documente", page "Import angajați", over five rounds; round 5 was approved on 2026-10-31 with nothing to flag. The canvas is the source of truth for what the screens show; this ADR decides what is behind them and replaces the employee import section of ADR 030 and steps 9 and 10 of its order of work. The decisions come from Mircea in conversation on 2026-10-30 and 2026-10-31.

What the law says of people who are already at work, read on 2026-10-30 in the primary and consolidated texts. They need no new training and no new test. HG 1425/2006 art. 83 gives the introductory general training only in four cases: at hiring, to workers seconded (_detașați_) or delegated (_delegați_) from another unit, and to a worker placed by a temporary agency; art. 89(1) and (2) close it with the test whose result the sheet records; art. 94 has the workplace training verified by the trainer's superior, and art. 96(2) and (4) the periodic one by the superior and, by sampling, the employer or the prevention service, with no periodic occupational safety test. For fire safety, OMAI 712/2005 art. 14 and 20 test the hiring trainings, art. 15 keeps from the workplace those who fail, and art. 30 tests everyone in periodic training once a year, the results recorded in the individual sheet; art. 39 lit. c) has a move to another employer count as a new hire. Digitising a sheet, or changing provider, is none of these: no act makes it a hiring. The paper sheet stays the original. HG 1425/2006 art. 81(4) and (5) have it kept at the workplace until employment ends, OMAI 712/2005 art. 50 has the head of the workplace keep it, and no act provides for replacing a paper sheet with a transcription of it. What the app holds of it is therefore a transcription, never a replacement: _transcris de pe fișa pe hârtie_.

Keeping the sheet electronically from now on rests on HG 1425/2006 art. 81(1) to (3³), as amended by HG 259/2022, and on the Labour Code, art. 16(1²), (1⁶) and (1⁷) and art. 17(3) lit. o), (4) and (5), through which an employee already under contract agrees by an addendum: the **signing agreement** of [ADR 023](adr-023-advanced-electronic-signature.md), which stays. OMAI 712/2005 art. 47 still reads "cu pastă sau cu cerneală", which [ADR 021](adr-021-training-records-and-individual-sheets.md) noted and Mircea decided on 2026-10-16 not to follow for the fire-safety sheet either.

The data an import reads is processed under a legal obligation, GDPR art. 6(1) lit. c): the employer must keep the sheet, and the provider keeps it for the employer. The privacy notice informs; it does not ask, and a consent checkbox would misstate the basis (EDPB guidelines 05/2020, ¶21–22). Legea 190/2018 art. 4 is the national rule for processing the CNP. The blood group and Rh are health data under GDPR art. 9, already named in the data processing agreement and the privacy policy (#655).

How large a client is. An employer of up to 49 workers may rely on an external prevention service alone; from 50 to 249 it must also designate workers or have an internal service, and above 250 the internal service is mandatory, with external services as a complement (HG 1425/2006 art. 16 to 19). The provider's typical client has 2 to 30 people: 94.7% of Romanian firms employ 0 to 9 people and 4.4% 10 to 49 (Eurostat, 2024).

A model was evaluated on sheet photographs on 2026-10-30 (14 real photographs of two people, kept outside the repository with their consent for development only; report outside the repository). GPT-6.1 Terra, one request per page, read the page fields 100% right, the training rows 94%, placed 14 pages of 14 with the right person, at $0.0063 a page, about $0.04 a person, in 9.4 seconds a page. Downscaling to 1,024 pixels saved only 14% and misread more names. Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite read 100% and 92% at $0.0010 a page but got periodic dates and durations wrong; choosing a model per import kind is #703, later. The open-weight models served on Cloudflare could not transcribe handwriting. The 14 photographs carry no CNP, so reading a CNP is untested.

## Decision

### What it takes from ADR 030, and what is new

From ADR 030, unchanged: the import as a proposal nothing of which is written until the member confirms; the upload dialog, with files, a folder or a ZIP in one drop zone; the `imports` bucket; the extractor, through which every photograph is converted to an upright JPEG of at most 2,000 pixels a side with its metadata stripped (done for every import on 2026-10-31); the `ssm-usor-import` queue with its dead-letter consumer and the daily clean-up; the processing page, which counts scanned pages one by one; the model reached through Cloudflare AI Gateway with its cost stored per import and per file; the source dialog showing a photograph with zoom; the existing-client rule of variant E2, under which the app's value is kept unless the member takes the other; the retention of the proposal; and the data protection section. An employee import exists only for a client that exists and starts only from the client's _Angajați_ tab, which the `imports` table already requires of the `employees` kind.

New: the three ways to start on the _Angajați_ tab, with the sheet photographs a client import found; the reading of one sheet page per request with a schema of its own, the grouping of pages into people by code, the caps of an employee import, the review by person, the confirm function `apply_employee_import`, the paper test result read from a signature, the change to when a hiring test awaits and the test state _Neverificat pe fișă_, the paper rows' signature line in the app and the portal, and inviting the imported people to the portal at once.

### Starting: three ways, and the sheets a client import found

A documentation pack often holds photographs of training sheets. The client import sorts them as `training_sheet` and copies them into the client's import folder as plain files (ADR 030); the employee import offers them back, in two places (variants B and C of the canvas, boards FoundSheetsB, FoundSheetsC and FoundSheetsCPhone, decided by Mircea on 2026-10-31).

- **The _Angajați_ tab's entry** offers three ways: **Adaugă angajat**, by hand; **Importă din fișele găsite în documentație (N fotografii)**, which opens the upload with them already added; and **Importă din fotografii noi**. The second is shown only while the client has found sheet photographs no employee import has taken, whether or not the client has employees yet.
- **The upload dialog** of an employee import offers them too, "Din documentația importată pe <data>: N fotografii de fișe · Adaugă-le", so a member who started from new photographs still finds them.

A found photograph added to an import is read, grouped and kept as an uploaded one is. A client file shared with the owners only is never offered, and a photograph the documentation holds twice, by its sha256, is offered once.

### Reading: one page, one request

Every photograph and every PDF page is read on its own by GPT-6.1 Terra, with medium reasoning, as `scanPages` of the client import reads a scanned page. The page's kind comes from the same answer, so the employee import has no sorting step. The page's schema, held as structured output like the client import's:

- the **page kind**: SSM sheet front, SSM periodic table, PSI sheet front, PSI periodic table, commute declaration, other;
- the **identifiers** on the page: name, CNP, employee number;
- the **personal fields**: birth date and place, home address, education, qualification, blood group, contract title, hire date ("admis la lucru"), leave date, commute;
- the **training rows**: domain, phase, date, duration, trainer, the trainer's function, material, and for each signature box whether it is signed, empty or unclear. A date written as a month and a year only is kept as `YYYY-MM`, never filled in by the model;
- the **unreadable** fields, each named.

A page the gateway refuses for good stays unread, to be assigned by the member like a page code cannot place, and the rest of its file is read.

### Grouping pages into people, by code

The model reads pages; code groups them. A page goes to a person by the CNP, only one whose check digit holds, since a misread digit would split the person; without one, by the name folded to plain letters with a near-name rule: the surname within one edit, wherever the sheet writes it, and about two thirds of the name's parts shared, since exact names split one person into two in the evaluation. Given names that differ only by a final letter added or dropped, Daniel and Daniela, are two people. A page with neither CNP nor name goes by the employee number to the person whose pages carry it; without any identifier, a periodic continuation page most often, by the order of the upload, after the page before it, and is flagged _O pagină pusă după ordine_. A page code cannot place at all is offered to the member to assign to a person or to leave out; a page left out is no one's file and goes with the import. Pages of one person that disagree, two hire dates or two addresses, give a choice.

### What an employee import can hold

An employee import holds at most 500 photographs or PDF pages, 25 MiB a file and 1 GiB in all; a client import keeps the caps of ADR 030. A typical import is 50 to 60 people. JPEG and PNG photographs are made smaller in the browser before the upload, to about 2,000 pixels, which every browser can do, and are measured against the 25 MiB and the 1 GiB once made smaller, the dialog showing their total as an estimate; HEIC goes up as it is, since Chrome cannot decode it, and is converted by the extractor; PDF and HEIC are measured as chosen. The extractor reads a PDF of up to 1,000 pages. A larger client is several imports, each person's photographs best kept in one; a later import completes the employees an earlier one created, by the rule for existing employees below.

### The review: people are the unit

The hub lists people, not sections. A person is **De verificat**, **Verificat** or **Lăsat deoparte**. Opening a person with nothing to decide and moving to the next marks them _Verificat_; a person with a decision is checked once it is made, and goes back to _De verificat_ when an edit, a page given to them or a choice leaves them something to decide again. Every change is saved as it is made, as ADR 030's review saves; an import nobody touches is failed 14 days after its last change and deleted with its files 14 days after that, as every import is. The hub counts "23 din 40 verificați" and opens on the first person still to check; the person page shows the sheet's photographs in the narrow column of variant C (ADR 030). While an import is open, the client's _Angajați_ tab shows it with the day it expires and **Continuă**.

There is **one confirm**, at the end. Confirming while people are still _De verificat_ is allowed after a warning that names them, and they are left out; a new import can bring them. A partial confirm, creating the checked people now and the rest later, was rejected by Mircea: people who are urgent go in a small import of their own.

Per person:

- **To be created**, an employee needs a last name, a first name, a contract title, a hire date and a job position. An email or phone is optional and its absence is a quiet flag, "Fără email sau telefon — îl adaugi când îl inviți în portal", since only the portal invitation needs one.
- **The job position** is one of the client's, ideally those the client import brought. A contract title that matches a position's name exactly is assigned quietly; a near match is proposed, for the member to confirm; no match blocks, with the closest position suggested, an existing position offered first and **Adaugă un post nou** second, through the app's own position dialog. One unmatched title shared by several people is decided once for all of them. A client with no positions gets a notice suggesting the client import first, and the member may go on. A position is never created silently: the import always names the position, so the `default_employee_job_position` trigger, which creates a position named after the contract title for an employee inserted without one, never fires from it.
- **The workplace** is handled like the position (Mircea, 2026-10-31). A sheet's text that matches one of the client's workplaces is assigned quietly; a near match is proposed to confirm; one that matches none, or a sheet that names none, is asked on the person page, one answer for everyone with the same text; a client with one workplace is never asked. A question left unanswered keeps the person _De verificat_, but the answer may be no workplace for now: the employee is created and shows it as missing sheet data, as the app does today.
- **The CNP** is checked by its checksum and against the birth date; where one digit is unreadable and the birth date fixes it, the corrected CNP is offered to accept. A CNP whose check digit fails is used neither to group pages nor to match an employee, and the member may correct any CNP among the personal data. A blood group written "A II Rh+" is split into the group, `A(II)`, and the Rh, `+`, as the employee's columns hold them.
- **An employee the client already has**, by the CNP, else by the name and the birth date, is completed, not duplicated: empty fields are filled, and a value that differs from the app's is a choice with the app's value kept by default, as ADR 030's E2. A training already in the app, the same domain, phase and day, is hidden as a duplicate, with a count. A person with no valid CNP and no birth date is proposed as the one employee of the same name, word order aside (`name_only`), and the member confirms or declines it: a later import's continuation pages often carry the name alone. Two people of an import never complete the same employee; the second blocks the confirm.
- **A match to a former employee** is flagged. A sheet hire date after their leave date blocks, since in the app a rehire after a gap is a new employee and reactivating is for undoing a mistake; the import never reactivates. The member declines the match, which creates the person as a new employee with the CNP and the employee number kept on one row only, as for any rehire; or corrects the date; or leaves the person out.
- **A leaver**, a sheet with a leave date, is proposed unticked, and imported as a former employee if ticked. The default follows the leave date as the member leaves it, so clearing it proposes the person ticked.

**Every training row is imported**, the whole history, which Mircea wants for its legal weight, not only the minimum the due dates need. A month-only date becomes the first of the month, tagged _Doar luna pe fișă_. A missing trainer, trainer's function or material is taken from the client's training themes and trainer, tagged _Din tematica clientului_. A row filled in and not signed is proposed unticked, tagged _Nesemnat pe fișă_. A duration written in hours, "8 ore" or "1h30", is read as minutes. Two checks flag a row and never block it: an unusual duration, and a date before the hire date. An unusual duration is a periodic one other than 60, 90 or 120 minutes, an occupational safety hiring or supplementary training under the hour HG 1425/2006 art. 80¹ sets for every phase, or a fire-safety one under 15 minutes, which catches hours read as minutes (see Open). A date after today blocks: the database refuses a training held in the future (`trainings_held_on_past`). "Admis la lucru", its day, name and function, goes on the workplace training record, the only one that holds it.

**Rows become trainings held.** Rows of several people whose domain, phase, date, trainer, duration and material all match become one training held with all of them as participants, as the app records a training held once with its attendees (ADR 021). Rows that differ in any of them become separate trainings held on the same day: a training held has one duration and one material, and the periodic material differs per position. The member decides nothing about it (Mircea, 2026-10-31).

### Tests: a verifier's signature on a hiring block is the hiring test, passed on paper

The sheet's hiring block is closed by the test (HG 1425/2006 art. 89; OMAI 712/2005 art. 14 and 20), and the verifier signs it once the test is passed. So a signed verifier box on a hiring block becomes a **test result** from paper ([ADR 024](adr-024-knowledge-tests.md)), marked _Verificat pe fișă_, dated the day of that hiring training as the member leaves it, with the verifier as the examiner, else the trainer where the form has no verifier box: the occupational safety result from the SSM block, the fire-safety hiring result from the PSI block. The form has no date of its own for the test, so it follows its row: the newest signed hiring row among the pages a person holds once the member has moved pages, and a row dated in the future blocks the test with it.

**When a hiring test awaits changes.** In ADR 024 a hiring test awaits any employee with a hiring training record in the domain and no pass, computed by `employee_test_due` from the introductory general record for occupational safety and the workplace record for fire safety. Now it awaits only when the newest of those records was held in the app (`trainings.source = 'app'`). An employee whose newest hiring record of the domain is from paper and who has no pass recorded owes nothing: no count on the dashboard, nothing in the portal.

**Their test state is a value of its own, _Neverificat pe fișă_** (`unverified` in the `test_state` enum), grey like _Fără test_, which keeps meaning that no hiring training is recorded (Mircea, 2026-10-31), rather than widened to take people trained on paper. It shows in the employee lists, the client's and the organization's, and is an option of their test-state filter; it is never counted on the dashboard and never shown in the portal, where nothing awaits the employee. The employee's page says it in full: "Testul la angajare nu a putut fi verificat pe fișă: semnătura nu se vede clar sau lipsește." The wording says the signature could not be seen, not that it is missing, since a photograph may simply not show it. The member can still record a paper result there, which makes the state _promovat_. The rule follows the training's source, so it applies just as much to trainings typed from a paper sheet by hand.

**The fire-safety annual test** is imported where the sheet records it, as a row of the PSI periodic table, which is where art. 30 has it recorded and where the app prints it: such a row becomes an annual paper result, not a training, and one without a readable date or examiner is asked for them. The API refuses a paper annual result without an earlier pass today (`apps/api/src/modules/tests/handlers.ts`, `recordPaperTestResult`); that refusal goes, for the import and for a member typing one. Where the sheet records no annual test, it is due twelve months after the last pass and shows as _anual scadent_: a yearly duty the employer really has, shown as it is (agreed by Mircea).

### Confirming, and what follows

Confirming runs `apply_employee_import`, one transaction like `apply_client_import`, which only inserts and refuses any import whose kind is not `client`. It creates or completes the employees; creates the trainings held, source paper, with their records and the admission; inserts the paper test results; and creates the employees' files. Then the API brings the stored signed sheets up to date with the new records (`syncTrainingRows`, which runs in the API, not the database).

**Signatures.** A paper training asks nobody to sign: the signature requests already return early for a training whose source is paper (`apps/api/src/modules/signing/requests.ts`). What is new is that a paper row says so. In the app's training rows and in the portal it reads "Transcris de pe fișa pe hârtie · Semnat pe hârtie", where today the app shows a badge _De pe fișa de hârtie_ and no signature line, and the portal nothing; the signed sheet already prints _semnat pe hârtie_ in a paper row's note. A paper training printed on a signed sheet, which no longer changes, is locked in the app with a message of its own, not the one that asks to cancel electronic signatures it does not have.

**"Pe hârtie", everywhere.** The app says _fișa pe hârtie_, never _fișa de hârtie_ (Mircea, 2026-10-31): the badge becomes _Transcris de pe fișa pe hârtie_, the training's source _Preluată de pe fișa pe hârtie_, and the training dialog's hint and the help follow.

**Files.** Each photograph's converted copy, upright and about half a megabyte, becomes a client file of the employee it belongs to, in a folder per import, _Fișe importate pe <data>_, on the employee's _Date_ tab, the way ADR 030's import folder groups a client's files. The original goes with the import: removed 14 days after an import is abandoned or failed, and 30 days after it is confirmed, once its proposal is dropped and its files copied, as the clean-up does today (`apps/api/src/modules/imports/cleanup.ts`). A scanned PDF holding several people's sheets gives each employee only their own pages, as the JPEGs the import read them from, _<name> — pagina N.jpg_, in their folder; the PDF itself stays once among the client's files, in the import's folder in _Alte documente_, unless it came from the client's documentation pack, where it already is. A PDF holding one person's sheets follows the same rule. Client files take up to 25 MiB, as an employee import's files do. Storage's global upload limit is 25 MiB too, and each bucket keeps its own limit under it.

**The signing agreement.** Imported employees are existing employees, so they sign the act adițional of ADR 023 before they sign electronically; the import never marks it `in_contract`, which is for a new hire whose contract holds the clause. Its paper version still needs an email or phone to be downloaded, as today (`employee_has_no_address`): the contacts a sheet lacks are collected after the import, the specialist calling or writing to the employer and filling them in (Mircea, 2026-10-31).

### Inviting the imported people to the portal, at once

Confirming invites nobody: the portal invitation stays a member's explicit act. What is new is that the member can invite the imported people in one act instead of one at a time, which ADR 022 left out until a client wanted it (canvas board EmpInvite, "5-7. Invitare în portal a angajaților din fișe", decided by Mircea on 2026-10-31).

- **Where.** A dialog, **Invită în portal**, offered once the import is confirmed, and again from the notice the client's _Angajați_ tab shows after an import while some of the people it brought are neither invited nor in the portal. The notice shows the newest of the client's last 10 confirmed imports that still has someone uninvited; hiding it hides that import's notice only, and the next one shows.
- **Whom.** It lists the import's employees who are current: leavers imported as former employees are not listed. An employee who is already a participant or has an open invitation is shown as such and not sent anything again; their resend stays on their page and the Portal tab.
- **The address, typed in place.** Each row holds one field with the employee's email, else their phone, from their row, and takes an email or a phone typed over it or into it where the sheet gave none. What is typed decides the channel: an email address goes by email, a phone number by SMS. The typed address is saved to the employee, by the same rules as editing the employee, the phone normalized to E.164, in one transaction with the invitation: an invitation refused leaves the employee unchanged. An address that cannot be saved or normalized is shown on its row and that person is not invited. A row left empty is a person left uninvited, with nothing blocking the others.
- **One action** sends every invitation. Each is the ADR 022 invitation as it is, through `create_participant_invitation` and the same send: one open invitation per address in a client, valid 30 days, the auth account created at send time, the message by the mail Worker or the SMS Worker, and a send that fails leaving the invitation saved for a resend after 10 minutes. The invitations are independent: one refused, an address already a participant's for another person, say, is named on its row and does not stop the rest, and the dialog ends with how many were sent and which were not.
- **Costs and caps.** An SMS invitation and its first code cost about $0.16 to a Romanian number (ADR 022), so the dialog says how many go by SMS before sending; an import of 50 people without email is about $8. The invitations themselves go out through the Workers, outside Supabase's limits, but the sign-in codes that follow fall under the project's hourly caps on codes, 100 SMS and 60 emails, sized in ADR 022 for a floor of employees signing in on one afternoon; they stay as they are.

The invited employee meets in the portal what ADR 022 and ADR 023 give any employee: the privacy notice first, then the act adițional to sign, then the trainings to come, which they sign there. Their imported paper rows show as transcribed and signed on paper, with nothing for them to sign.

### Where the build departs from the canvas

These differences in behaviour were accepted when the screens were built (Mircea, 2026-10-31), the last three after the stack's review:

- **Adaugă un post nou** creates the position at once, through the app's position dialog, and assigns it; it is not held in the proposal until confirming.
- An import whose people are read and ready takes no more photographs. Sheets that turn up later go in a new import, which completes the same employees by the rule for existing employees.
- A near match between a contract title and a position keeps the person _De verificat_ until the member confirms the proposed position.
- An unanswered workplace question, a near match included, keeps the person _De verificat_ too.
- A page with neither CNP nor name is grouped by the employee number.
- A page placed by the upload's order is flagged _O pagină pusă după ordine_, not _Plasată după ordine_.

### Usage

The reading is recorded per photograph or page from the first import, with `record_import_usage` against the file, as the client import's scans are. Credits are #640.

### What this changes in earlier ADRs

- **ADR 030**: its employee import section and steps 9 and 10 of its order of work are replaced by this ADR; the caps of its defaults stay the client import's; the sheet photographs a client import sorts as `training_sheet` are offered to an employee import.
- **ADR 024**: a hiring test awaits only after a hiring training record held in the app; after a paper hiring record without a pass, the test state is a fifth value, _Neverificat pe fișă_, in the lists and their filter but never on the dashboard or in the portal, and the employee's page says why; a verifier's signature on a paper hiring block is a paper result; and a paper annual result may be recorded without an earlier pass.
- **ADR 021**: training rows from paper show their signature line, _Transcris de pe fișa pe hârtie · Semnat pe hârtie_, in the app and the portal, and their badge reads _Transcris de pe fișa pe hârtie_; the extraction its "History kept on paper" section left for later is this import.
- **ADR 023**: nothing changes. Existing employees keep the act adițional, and its paper version still needs an email or phone (#712).
- **ADR 022**: the bulk invitation it left out is built for the people of an employee import: the same invitation, sent to many in one act, with each address typed in place saved to the employee together with its invitation.
- **ADR 013**: an employee's files can come from an import, grouped in a folder per import on the employee's page, as the client's are in "Alte documente"; a client file takes up to 25 MiB instead of 20 MiB, one limit with an employee import's files.

## What is left out

- REGES-Online exports as a source of employees, and spreadsheets.
- Choosing the reading model per import kind (#703).
- A partial confirm.
- Credits and their allowances (#640).
- The act adițional on paper for an employee without email or phone (#712): its download keeps requiring one, and the contacts are collected after the import.
- Inviting at once employees who did not come from an import.
- A mark on the trainings, test results and employee data an import read, telling what a model read from what a member typed (#715): today both read _Transcris de pe fișa pe hârtie_.

## Consequences

- An import can be of two kinds with two sets of caps: the file trigger, the bucket's size limit, the contracts' limits and the scanned-page cap become per kind.
- The test state's rule depends on a training's source for the first time, and the dashboard's count of pending tests, which reads the same view, follows it. The `test_state` enum gains a value, so every place that reads it, the contracts, the generated clients, the lists, the filter and the portal, has a fifth case to handle.
- A paper result can exist with no paper test behind it but a signature on the sheet; support reads the photograph in the employee's files to see why.
- The employee lists, the dashboard and the portal stop asking imported employees for hiring tests they took on paper years ago, and start showing the annual fire-safety test due for those whose sheet records none.
- `packages/import` gains a second schema and prompt, and grouping by code, tested on invented sheets and recorded answers; the reference photographs stay outside this public repository.
- The pull request that completes the feature, the bulk invitation, carries its `## Actualizări` block; the help articles on trainings and tests and on the portal and the act adițional change along the stack.
- A client's portal can fill in one afternoon: an import of 50 people without email is about $8 of SMS invitations and first codes, and the hourly caps on sign-in codes are what a whole floor signing in at once meets first.

## Order of work

Each step is a pull request in one stack, on this ADR, in the order below. As first planned, the app came sixth, before the after-import displays, and the sheet files, decided on 2026-10-31 while the stack was being built, were not a step; they sit before the app, which shows them.

1. This ADR, the notes in ADR 024 and ADR 030, and the glossary in `CONTEXT.md`.
2. The schema and the test rule: the caps per kind in the `limit_import_files` trigger, the `imports` bucket's size limit, `importLimits` in `packages/contracts` and `maxScannedPages` in the imports pipeline; `employee_test_due` awaiting a hiring test only after a record held in the app and returning the new `unverified` value of the `test_state` enum after a paper hiring record without a pass, with `testStates` and `testStateFilters` in `packages/contracts` and the dashboard's count leaving it out; the paper annual result allowed in `apps/api/src/modules/tests`. pgTAP and API tests.
