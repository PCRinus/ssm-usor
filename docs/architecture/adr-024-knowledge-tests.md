# ADR 024: Knowledge tests as a question bank, taken in the portal and recorded per employee

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-10-20

## Context

The document packs hold the knowledge tests as documents: the occupational safety set has a test at hiring (5.1, coded TASSM) and a periodic test (5.2, TPSSM), the fire-safety set a test at hiring (4.1, TASU) and an annual test (4.2, TPSU). Each is a Word file with the questions, a table for the name, the date, the two signatures and "Rezultatul testării: Admis / Respins", an empty answer grid, and then the same questions again with the key, so the employee's copy and the answers travel in one file. Ten, fourteen, twelve and ten multiple-choice questions, printed, filled by hand, kept in the binder. The questions are the same for every client whatever its activity, which the SSM contact confirmed on 2026-10-20: the tests are static in practice. They map to the common parts, the general training material 2.2, the chapters of the own instructions 3.2 and the IPSU, never to the provider's instruction modules, and a few no longer map to anything after the first-aid rewrite of 2026-10-07; one of 5.1's questions depends on eight pictograms in formats a browser does not render. Mircea asked on 2026-10-16 (#449) to rethink them as something interactive, served apart from the documentation, with the result recorded per employee, and left the test block of the SSM sheet empty until this was decided ([ADR 021](adr-021-training-records-and-individual-sheets.md)).

What the law asks, read on 2026-10-20 in the consolidated texts. For occupational safety, HG 1425/2006 art. 89(1): "Instruirea introductiv-generală se va finaliza cu verificarea însușirii cunoștințelor pe bază de teste", the result recorded in the sheet of annex 11 or electronically (alin. 2), and a new hire who has not acquired the knowledge "nu va putea fi angajat" (alin. 3). The workplace training ends with a check by the trainer's superior, not a test (art. 94). The periodic training is verified by the hierarchical superior of whoever gave it and, by sampling, by the employer or the prevention service, at the periodicity the client's training-testing programme sets (art. 96(2) and (4)); no article asks for a periodic test. Annex 11 has a block "Rezultatele testărilor" with four columns, Data, Materialul examinat, Calificativ, Examinator, and no signature column. Art. 81(3³) obliges the employer to keep the materials of an electronic training traceable and intact. For fire safety, OMAI 712/2005 art. 14 tests the introductory general training, art. 20 admits to work "numai după verificarea acestora pe bază de teste", art. 15 keeps those who fail from the workplace, and art. 30 has "anual … o verificare de fond pe bază de teste tip chestionar", its results recorded in the individual sheet, whose form has no test block at all. Art. 58 pct. V asks a test to hold enough questions from the material used, with its scoring, its grids and its instructions. No act sets a number of questions, a pass mark or a form, and none says what follows a failed periodic or annual test. The provider's own fire-safety decision 1.2 closes each hiring training with "un test grilă de minimum 10 întrebări", 70% of the answers right to pass, and says that those who fail "vor repeta testarea până vor fi declarate apte". OUG 32/2026 art. 7(1) lit. d) obliges an employer of non-EU workers to train them "într-o limbă pe care străinul o înțelege", which the portal's second language will have to serve one day (#532).

What the competitor does. ssm.ro attaches a question bank to each uploaded instruction, three options with one correct, written by hand, generated with a model or imported from a spreadsheet; a position's test draws a set number of questions from the instructions of its themes, and a separate annual test draws at random across the periodic themes. The employee reads each material in the browser, answers its questions, then signs the sheet. The pass mark, the attempts and the shuffling are settings of its optional course application, and nothing says what a failed legal test does. There is no built-in question library.

What the app holds. The training records of ADR 021, with the hiring and periodic trainings per employee and a test block that prints five blank rows. The portal of [ADR 022](adr-022-participants-and-the-portal.md), which left "answers to a test" as the first thing a participant would write. The signature of [ADR 023](adr-023-advanced-electronic-signature.md), whose pipeline was built so that "only the document and the slots differ" when the tests come. Two libraries, instructions ([ADR 012](adr-012-own-instructions.md)) and risks ([ADR 015](adr-015-risk-assessment.md)), both starting empty by decision, so there is no precedent yet for content the app ships and the provider edits. The product scope, §6.5, which promised a question bank with a threshold, attempts and feedback, and §4.5, where an employee completes "a test and sees whether another attempt is permitted".

Mircea decided on 2026-10-20, after the five questions of #449 were researched: the periodic occupational safety test leaves the pack, since no law asks for it and the app keeps what the law needs; the pass mark is 70% with free retakes; a missing hiring pass shows as a gap and blocks nothing; the hiring tests appear for the employee as soon as the hiring training is recorded, the annual test follows the date; every question may carry a short explanation, optional on the provider's own; the tests are served in Romanian only; questions attached to the provider's instruction modules (#531), translations (#532) and explanations written by a model (#533) are later ideas; and no approval of IGSU is sought under OMAI 712/2005 art. 65, since the content is the provider's, who may discard every seeded question (#388).

## Decision

### Three tests, by law; the periodic occupational safety test leaves the pack

A **knowledge test** (_test de verificare a cunoștințelor_) is one of three: the occupational safety **hiring test** (_testul la angajare SSM_, TASSM), which closes the introductory general training under HG 1425/2006 art. 89; the fire-safety hiring test (_testul la angajare SU_, TASU), which closes both hiring trainings under OMAI 712/2005 art. 14 and 20, as [ADR 019](adr-019-fire-safety-stage-three.md) read them (#386); and the fire-safety **annual test** (_testul anual SU_, TPSU) of art. 30. The periodic occupational safety test is dropped: the template 5.2 and its type leave the set, the cover 5.0 names the one test, the article of the own instructions that announced a yearly test coded TPSSM says instead that the periodic training is verified by the hierarchical superior of whoever gave it and, by sampling, by the employer or the prevention service, as art. 96(4) has it, the training themes 4.2 lose "Testare." from the content line of the last periodic session, and the employer's briefing 11 no longer lists it among the documents handed over. The verification the themes announce for the hiring training, "Testare conform TASSM", stays. Keeping 5.2 as an option per client was rejected: the app is sold to many providers, not built around one habit, and a test nobody has to pass is a document nobody has to keep.

### A question bank per organization, seeded with the built-in questions

The organization holds a **question bank** (_banca de întrebări_), a library beside the instructions and the risks: **test questions** (_întrebări_), each of one domain and one test, hiring or annual, with its text, two to four options of which one or more are correct, an optional **explanation** (_explicație_) of two or three sentences in plain Romanian, and an optional citation of the article it rests on. The bank is seeded: the questions of the three tests ship as **built-in questions** (_întrebări predefinite_), rows with no organization, read by every organization and edited by none, rewritten where the sample's were broken, the pictogram question as text, each with an explanation and its article. A provider **hides** a built-in question it does not want and restores it later, and writes **own questions** (_întrebări proprii_) beside them, with the explanation optional; an own question may name the built-in it replaces, as provenance only, so that the provider can see what it departed from. Editing a built-in in place was rejected: a corrected built-in must reach every provider through "Șablon actualizat", which only works if the rows are the app's; the hide-and-replace rule gives the provider the same freedom without the merge. Both libraries of the organization start empty by decision; this one does not, because every provider in the trade tests from the same ten generic questions, and a provider who disagrees hides them in a minute.

The content is the provider's. The app shows a question to the provider exactly as the employee will see it, the explanation after a wrong answer included, and judges nothing about whether a safety statement is true: a wrong own question is the provider's mistake, as a wrong module is. A **sitting** (_o susținere_) is ten questions of the test, drawn at random from its active questions and shuffled, options included; the **pass mark** is 70%, seven of the ten answered fully right. A test keeps at least ten active questions: hiding a built-in is refused when it would leave fewer, with a message as archiving a module in use gets, so that the provider adds before it hides. The seeded sets hold ten, twelve and ten questions, at least the ten the provider's own fire-safety decision promises; a bank that grows makes every retake a different test, which is what keeps a pass meaningful without a cooldown. Ten and 70% are fixed for every organization; a setting is #535, when a provider asks.

### The test documents are generated from the bank, and stay the paper path

The three test documents stay in their sets, generated with the rest and printed when needed. What changes is where their questions come from. Today each test is a Word file with its questions typed in as fixed text, and generating it fills the header alone. Now the questions live in the bank, and the template keeps its layout, the title, the name and date table, "Admis / Respins", the answer grid and the specimen with the key, with a repeating block where the questions were typed, as 3.2's table of positions and 4.2's table of sessions repeat a row per item. Generating the test prints every active question of the bank in that block, and the key from the same rows; the revision's snapshot records the questions as printed, so an edit in the bank shows "Date modificate" on the draft and regenerating prints the bank as it is. The document prints the whole bank, never a draw: a document must come out the same each time it is generated, and the binder should show every question the provider uses. A provider whose bank grows to thirty questions prints a long test, by its own choice.

Keeping the typed files was rejected: a provider who hides two questions and writes three would have its employees take one test in the portal and its binder hold another. Taking the tests out of the sets was rejected: the themes cite them by code, the fire-safety decision describes them, ITM asks for "testele utilizate" beside the programme and the themes, and an inspector reads a binder. The printed test is also the path for a client whose workers have no phones or no invitation yet: the specialist prints it, the employee fills it by hand, all of its questions, and the specialist records the pass in the app as a paper result, below.

### A test awaits the employee by itself

A test is never sent. The portal's _Acasă_ lists it under what needs the participant, beside the signature requests, from the moment the condition holds, and the condition is computed, as the training state is, never stored:

- The hiring test of a domain awaits an employee who has a hiring training record in it and no pass: for occupational safety from the introductory general record, for fire safety from the workplace record, since its one test closes both hiring trainings. The specialist records the training, the same day or days later, and the test is there.
- The annual fire-safety test awaits an employee twelve months after their last fire-safety pass, hiring or annual, and stays until passed. No training and no signature gates it: the sheet is never "fully signed", it grows by a row at every training, and a signature can lag a training by days.

From the condition, a **test state** (_starea testării_) per domain, shown on the employee's page, on the client's employees list and in the portal, beside the training state: none before a hiring training is recorded, _de susținut_ while a test awaits, _promovat_ once passed and, for fire safety, until the year runs out, _anual scadent_ once it has. An employee with no portal has the same states, the test awaiting them on paper. A hiring pass blocks nothing in the app: the law keeps an untested new hire from work, and the app shows the gap where the sheet and the admission are shown and leaves enforcing it to the employer, as the unsigned agreement of ADR 023 warns and does not block. Telling the employee by email or SMS that a test awaits them is the notifications work, with the due trainings and the unsigned requests.

### Taking the test is lenient, and every attempt is kept

The portal's test page opens with the **study card** (_ce trebuie să știi_): the explanations of the test's questions that have one, in one screen, and under it the issued PDF of the material the test closes, the general training material for occupational safety and the fire-safety own instructions for fire safety, which the portal shows for the first time. The card is the study material: the two documents run to eighty and sixty pages of transcribed articles, which nobody reads on a phone to answer ten questions, and the test is meant to be passed by someone who attended the training. Then the test: one question per screen, the options as whole-row targets, a "3 din 10" counter, no timer, each answer saved as it is given so that a dropped connection resumes the **test attempt** (_încercarea_) where it stopped. At the end the result: the score, _promovat_ or _nepromovat_, and for a failed attempt each question answered wrong with its explanation, the correct option not marked, and the retake, at once, as many times as it takes, drawn and shuffled again. A cooldown, an attempt cap and a timer were rejected: the test is a legal formality the employee must get through, not a filter, and the provider's own decision already promises repetition until passed.

An attempt is a row: the participant, the domain and the kind, when it started and finished, the questions and options as shown with the answers chosen, the score and whether it passed, the language, the revision of the material shown, and the client address and user agent. The row is written when the attempt starts, with the questions as drawn and no key, takes each answer as it is given, and at the finish gains the key, the score and the explanations and is frozen, as the evidence rows of ADR 023 are, which is what art. 81(3³)'s traceability asks: what the employee saw and answered is in the row, whatever the bank becomes. Versioning the bank was rejected for this reason: a question edited after a thousand attempts needs no history of its own when every attempt carries its copy.

### A pass is a test result, printed on the sheet and signed as a test sheet

The passing attempt gives a **test result** (_rezultatul testării_): the employee, the domain and the kind, the day, the material examined as text, "Test de verificare a cunoștințelor la angajare (TASSM)" and the like, the grade, _admis_, and the **examiner** (_examinatorul_), proposed as the specialist for occupational safety and the fire-safety technician for fire safety, the people who give the introductory trainings, and replaceable. A result also comes from paper: the specialist records a test taken on the printed copy, with the day, the grade and the examiner, the source _de pe hârtie_, and no attempt behind it; a failed paper test is not recorded, since the sheet prints passes and the next sitting is the one that counts. On the occupational safety sheet the results fill the block "Rezultatele testărilor", one row per pass, with the four columns of annex 11; on the fire-safety sheet, whose form has no such block, the annual pass prints as a row of the periodic table with the material "Verificare anuală pe bază de test (TPSU): admis", the way art. 30 has it recorded there and the provider's paper does. On a signed sheet both are field fills, as a training's row is. A row of the periodic table for a test departs from ADR 021's rule that a row is a training that happened; a test that happened is the one other event the form has nowhere else to put.

The attempt itself is signed, as the paper test is: the passing attempt renders a **test sheet** (_fișa de test_), one PDF with the questions, the answers chosen, the score, the grade and two signer slots, the trainee's and the examiner's, kept with the employee's own documents beside the signing agreement and the two sheets, and signed through the pipeline of ADR 023, from the portal by the employee and from the app by the examiner, with a paper slot where the examiner has no account. The portal lists it to the employee after the pass. Signing the row of the sheet instead was rejected: annex 11's test block has no signature column, the fire-safety sheet has no block, and the paper practice signs the test itself.

### In the app and the portal

- The sidebar gains _Teste_, the bank: the questions by domain and test, built-in and own, hidden ones apart, the preview as the employee sees it, hiding and restoring, writing and editing own questions, and the count of active questions and the pass mark of each test, shown, not set.
- The employee's page, under _Instruire_, per domain: the test state, the results with their day, grade, examiner and source, the attempts with their score, the signed test sheet, and the paper result entry.
- The client's employees list and the organization's _Angajați_ page show the test state beside the training states and filter by it.
- The documents tabs lose 5.2 and show the three tests generated from the bank; the SSM and PSI sheets print the results.
- The portal: _Acasă_ lists the awaiting test; _Instruiri_ gains the test page with the card, the material, the attempt, the result and the retake, and lists the signed test sheet; the client administrator sees the test state per employee.

## What is left out

- The periodic occupational safety test in any form, per client or otherwise.
- Questions on the provider's instruction modules and a position's test drawn from them (#531); the sitting size and the pass mark as settings of the organization (#535).
- Translations of the questions and the portal's second language on the test (#532); explanations proposed by a model (#533).
- Telling the employee or the specialist that a test awaits or is overdue: the notifications work.
- Images in questions; importing and exporting questions; questions per client.
- A failed attempt's consequences: the app records it and nothing follows.
- Taking a test in the app on the employee's behalf: the paper result is the only entry by hand.
- Signing the test sheet by anyone but the trainee and the examiner; a test on the client administrator.
- A test tied to a specific periodic training, or an annual occupational safety test.

## Consequences

- The app ships content a provider edits for the first time: built-in rows without an organization, hidden or replaced per organization, which is the pattern a starter set of instruction modules or risk profiles would follow if it ever comes.
- A document type is retired: 5.2 leaves the contracts, the manifest, the sections and the engine, and the revisions of it that hosted holds, all test data, go with it.
- The three test templates become merge templates with a loop over questions, and their citations move from the templates' citation list to the questions' own; the themes' content line changes and the guard snapshots with it.
- A participant writes for the first time: the attempt, through routes of the portal's own. The draw, each answer and the finish are database functions that check the caller is the participant, never an insert policy, since a participant must not read the questions, whose options carry the key; policies let a participant read their own attempts and results and nobody else's.
- The signing pipeline signs a second document kind: requests on a test sheet beside requests on a training record, with the same identities, sessions and evidence, which ADR 023 anticipated.
- The portal reads issued document files for the first time: a manifest flag marks the two materials for the portal and a Storage policy lets a participant read their client's issued revision of them.
- Four more states to compute per employee and domain, on the lists and the page, after the training states.
- The glossary gains knowledge test, hiring test, annual test, question bank, test question, built-in question, own question, explanation, pass mark, test state, study card, test attempt, test result, examiner and test sheet; `docs/data-model.md`, `docs/api.md`, `docs/document-engine.md` and `docs/portal.md` change with the pull requests.

## Defaults taken instead of asking the provider

Settled with Mircea on 2026-10-20:

1. A sitting is ten questions drawn at random, the pass mark is 70%, a test keeps at least ten active questions, retakes are free and immediate, drawn and shuffled again.
2. The examiner is the specialist for occupational safety and the fire-safety technician for fire safety, replaceable on the result.
3. The fire-safety hiring test awaits the employee from the workplace training record, one test for both hiring trainings (#386).
4. The annual fire-safety test is due twelve months after the last fire-safety pass.
5. The occupational safety results print in annex 11's test block; the fire-safety annual pass prints as a row of the periodic table.
6. The material examined prints the test's name and code.
7. No approval under OMAI 712/2005 art. 65 is sought: the questions are the provider's, seeded or not.
8. The questions without a source in the current material and the pictogram question are rewritten in the seed and listed in the pull request for review.
