# ADR 020: The fire-safety documents of an operator from ten employees: a headcount gate, the binder's order, the site sheet and the first dated document

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-10-11

## Context

[ADR 016](adr-016-fire-safety-documents.md), [ADR 018](adr-018-fire-safety-means.md) and [ADR 019](adr-019-fire-safety-stage-three.md) built the fire-safety set: nine decisions, the own instructions, the themes, the tests, the list of means, the posted sheet and the registers, 24 templates, for every client whatever its headcount. ADR 019 left out the shorter list of OMAI 163/2007 art. 20 for clients under ten employees, the half-yearly evaluation report, the list of dangerous substances and the evacuation plan; the list of what the norms ask and the set lacks was drawn up on 2026-10-11.

OMAI 163/2007 was re-read that day in its consolidated text. Art. 17 lists the acts of authority of the administrator, art. 18 the documents and records an operator keeps, and art. 20 (1) applies both "la operatorii economici şi la instituţiile care au un număr de salariaţi cel puţin egal cu cel stabilit, conform legii, pentru întreprinderile mici", which ADR 018 read as ten employees under Legea 346/2004. Art. 20 (2) gives an operator under that headcount a shorter list of acts: a) the instructions and the employees' duties at the workplaces, b) open fire and smoking, c) the training, d) the stop-work disposition when a fire-safety permit is annulled, e) the rules for dangerous substances specific to its products. Art. 20 (3) gives its documents and records: a) the technical documentation, b) the permits and authorizations, c) the certificates of the means, d) the fire-work permit register, e) the organization of fire defence at the workplace, f) the training sheets, g) the list of dangerous substances, h) the control reports and the measures taken. Art. 18 alone asks for the site sheet (b, _fişa obiectivului_, with a copy sent to the inspectorate), the annual evaluation report (c), the installation registers (g), the protection plans (k), the records of exercises (l, m), the maintenance and check schedules (q) and the own control reports (r).

Two more provider packs were read on 2026-10-11 beside the gelato bar's: the pack of an auto workshop from 2016 and the pack of a construction firm from 2025, both from the same provider. Neither has a document type the set lacks, and both IPSUs cite art. 20 (2) as their basis while the packs hold all nine decisions. The construction firm works on temporary sites with no floor area, which ADR 018's readiness cannot pass. The provider also handed over a blank _fişa obiectivului_ with its filling instructions: the model of annex 4 of the regulation approved by OMAI 89/2013 (art. 34 (2)), which replaced the OMAI 1474/2006 annex 5 that art. 18 lit. b) still names. The model has about 74 rows: the operator's identity and points of work, the technician, general facts of the premises (year, area, levels, classification, structure, headcount, use), per building its constructive facts and the state of 26 kinds of installation and means, the dangerous substances, and how fire defence is organized.

The SSM contact said on 2026-10-11 that these documents are required, that the provider has no method for them yet, that the technician prepares the site sheet, the annual report, the substances list and the schedules, and that the plans and the fire risk analysis are not the technician's work. The decision of 2016 in the workshop's pack splits the duties the same way.

[ADR 010](adr-010-conditional-documents.md) already makes a document depend on the client's headcount: decision 1.5 of the occupational safety set exists from ten current employees, counted from the employee list, with "nu se aplică" and its reason on the tab, the count on the generation form, and a document that exists never hidden when the count falls.

The same day's review settled the rest with Mircea: workplaces that are not buildings, the substances list and the schedules are an amendment to ADR 018; the fire-safety instruction modules are an amendment to [ADR 012](adr-012-own-instructions.md); the training sheets wait for the per-employee documents of #183 (#425); the trainer rule of ADR 019 stays.

## Decision

### Documents an operator keeps from ten employees

A fire-safety document type can be required only from ten current employees, the gate of ADR 010 reused as it is. The count is the client's current employees in the app, leavers excluded, remote and field staff included, since the law counts employees and not seats. Under ten the readiness returns the type as not applicable, the _Documente PSI_ tab shows it as "nu se aplică" with the reason, cover 1.0 does not list it, and the generation form states the count. A document generated at ten or more stays, draft or issued, when the client falls under ten. There is no switch to include a gated document anyway, as ADR 010 has none: a provider who wants one for a small client is asking for a document the norm does not ask of that client, and the provider's own packs give no sign of it.

Of the 24 templates, three rest on art. 17 or 18 alone and are gated: the seasons decision (art. 17 lit. j)), the control decision (art. 18 lit. r) and art. 149 to 152) and the installation register 6.3 (art. 18 lit. g)). The others stay for every client:

The new documents of this ADR and of the ADR 018 amendment are gated the same way where art. 18 is their only basis: the schedules (lit. q)), the site sheet (lit. b)) and the annual report (lit. c)). The substances list is for every client, art. 20 (3) lit. g).

### The binder's order: conditional decisions last

The decisions are reordered so that the two gated ones come last: 1 _organizarea apărării împotriva incendiilor_, 2 _instruirea_, 3 _lucrul cu foc deschis_, 4 _fumatul_, 5 _cadrul tehnic_, 6 _instrucțiunile_, 7 _deșeurile_, 8 _perioadele caniculare și sezonul rece_, 9 _controlul propriu_. A decision's printed number stays the first PSI number plus its ordinal minus one (`fireDecisionOrdinals` in `apps/api/src/modules/documents/fire-safety.ts`), so a client under ten gets decisions 1 to 7 and a client from ten the same seven and then 8 and 9, with no renumbering and no gap. ADR 018 fixed the ordinals to the provider's binder so that a decision arriving later would not renumber the issued ones; this reorder is the one move that keeps that promise for every headcount from now on, and it happens while hosted holds test data only, so nothing issued is renumbered.

Type keys carry no number and do not change. What changes is the `number` of four manifest entries and their file names (1.5 → 1.8, 1.6 → 1.5, 1.7 → 1.6, 1.8 → 1.7), the ordinals map, the order of the decisions section on the _Documente PSI_ tab and in the contracts' type-key list, the citations of a decision by its number in other templates (the themes cite the posted instructions "by its number", ADR 019), and cover 1.0, which lists the decisions that exist, as ADR 010 did for the occupational safety cover. ADR 016's and ADR 018's numbering paragraphs get a dated note.

### The site sheet, from data where the data lives and as gaps where it does not

The **site sheet** (_fişa obiectivului_) is generated from the model of OMAI 89/2013 annex 4, not from the OMAI 1474/2006 annex 5 that art. 18 lit. b) still names, since the regulation of 2006 was replaced in 2013 and the inspectorates work with the current model. It is one document per client, 7.1 in the binder, type key `fire_site_sheet`, in a new section 7 of the tab, "Documente pentru inspectorat", and it is gated.

One rule decides which of its 74 rows become data: a row goes into data if it changes over time or another document needs it. Regeneration makes a fresh draft from the template without the manual edits (ADR 005), and art. 19 asks this sheet to be updated whenever the staff with duties, the premises or the installations change, so every row filled by hand in the editor is retyped at every update; the rows that change are therefore data, and the rows written once can be gaps. Modelling all 74 rows was rejected as a form nobody fills for a document filed once; generating it all as gaps was rejected because the first update would lose them all.

A fixed-space workplace gains an optional **building block**: the year built, the built area, the number of levels, the classification (_civilă_, _de producţie_, _de depozitare_, _cu funcţiuni mixte_), the structure, the heating fuel, whether gas is used and for what, the firm that maintains the electrical installation and its last check, the water reserve for extinguishing, and nothing a row of the inventory already holds: the detection and alarm, the safety lighting, the interior and exterior hydrants and the lightning protection print from `fire_installations`, with their maintainer and last check. One workplace is one building; a workplace that is a temporary or mobile site (ADR 018 amendment) has no building block and prints in the sheet as a point of work only. The block never blocks readiness: a client with an empty block gets a sheet with more gaps.

The rest of the sheet reads what the app holds. The identity rows come from the client and the organization; the points of work from the workplaces; the technician with the certificate from the organization; the extinguishers by type from `fire_equipment`; the installations from `fire_installations`; section 2, the dangerous substances, from the substances list of the ADR 018 amendment, or its "none" sentence; section 3, how fire defence is organized, from what the decisions already say: the alarm from decision 1, the training with its intervals and trainers from decision 2, the own control and its intervals from decision 9, the posted instructions from decision 6, the rules of order from decisions 3, 4 and 7, the maintenance from the inventory's maintainers, the fire-safety documents as the list of the set. The headcount per building prints the client's current count beside a gap, since employees are not assigned to workplaces. Every other row, the fire stability, the fire compartments, the access and evacuation routes, the stairs, the lifts, the ventilation, the smoke exhaust, the technological systems, the alarm type, the incident history, prints as a visible **gap** carrying the model's own filling instruction in brackets, the way ADR 007 prints the contract's price article for the owner to fill in the editor. If providers keep filling the same gap, that row is promoted to data.

The sheet names no addressee. Which inspectorate receives the copy of art. 18 lit. b) when a client's points of work span counties is the provider's question to its inspectorate, and the app does not pretend to know.

### Dated document types, and the annual report as the first

A document identity gains an optional **period**, a year, and the rule of ADR 005, one document type once per client, becomes once per client per period for a type that is dated. The _Documente PSI_ tab lists a dated type once per year, "Raport anual 2026", each year with its own draft and issued revisions, the current year open and earlier years folded under it; generating a dated type asks for the year, the current one proposed. Using revisions for the yearly reports was rejected: in the app a superseded revision is one that was replaced and is no longer in force, and a report of 2025 stays the report of 2025 beside the report of 2026, both asked for at a control.

The **annual evaluation report** (_raportul anual de evaluare a nivelului de apărare împotriva incendiilor_, art. 18 lit. c)) is the first dated type, 7.2 in the binder, type key `fire_annual_report`, gated. Its content follows art. 151 and 152: the analysis is held half-yearly or yearly, on a report of the structure with fire-safety duties, with the implementation of new legal provisions, the state of the measures set, the deficiencies, the conclusions of the training, the relations with third parties, the means and their state, the efficiency of the structures and the proposed measures. The template is a skeleton: the counts the app holds (the workplaces, the means by type, the installations and their checks, the posts trained by category, the decisions in force) are merged, and each section of art. 152 is a heading with a gap for the technician to fill in the editor. The fires, the controls and the exercises of the year are gaps too, since the app keeps no such records yet. The half-yearly analysis report that decision 9 promises uses the same mechanism later, with a half-year as its period.

### The trainer rule stays

ADR 019 names the trainer by staff category: the technician for the technical-administrative posts, the heads of the workplaces for the execution posts, as ADR 014 does for the occupational safety themes. The construction firm's pack has the administrator training an administrative post, which OMAI 712/2005 allows: art. 16 has the head of the workplace give the workplace training, and art. 31 (1) has the periodic training given to execution staff by the head of the workplace, to technical-administrative staff by the heads of their departments, and to the heads themselves by the technical manager. Art. 31 (2) goes further for an employer with at most nine employees: the periodic training is the employer's exclusive duty, so the themes of a client under ten should name the legal representative as the periodic trainer, which neither ADR 019 nor this ADR decides yet. The rule is kept and the case recorded in ADR 019 as a known deviation: a per-category choice would add a question to every client for one provider habit, and the better rule, the head of the workplace trains every post except the heads' own, would be a change to both sets at once, which is for a decision of its own.

## What is left out

- The evacuation plan and the intervention plan (art. 18 lit. k), art. 28 to 33 and annex 3): the evacuation plan is drawn, and a drawing editor for it is a feature of its own; the intervention plan reads the site sheet's data and needs the inspectorate's approval. Both wait for that feature, and a plan the provider has is uploaded as a client file ([ADR 013](adr-013-client-files.md)) meanwhile.
- The training sheets, one per employee: #425, with the per-employee documents of #183.
- The fire risk analysis (art. 18 lit. d)): the provider has no method, and it is not the technician's work.
- Fire-safety duties in job descriptions (art. 9), together with the occupational safety twin of #278.
- The half-yearly analysis report, the records of evacuation and intervention exercises (art. 18 lit. l), m)) and the own control reports (lit. r)): records of events, on the dated mechanism when they come.
- A switch to generate a gated document for a client under ten.
- Promoting any gap of the site sheet to data before a provider asks.
- The registration of a contract on the inspectorate's platform and the technician's authorization (ADR 019).

## Consequences

- The fire-safety set is no longer the same list for every client. A client under ten gets seven decisions, no installation register, and none of the four documents of section 7 and the schedules; the tab says so per document.
- A client that grows past ten gets decisions 8 and 9, the register, the schedules, the site sheet and the report at its next generation, with the numbers of 1 to 7 unchanged.
- Four template files are renamed and their manifest numbers move; the type keys, and so every stored document, stay. The tab's planned rows gain a section 7.
- The site sheet is the first document whose template prints gaps on purpose beside merged data, and the first that reads the substances list, the building block and the decisions' facts together; its snapshot marks it "Date modificate" on any change of them, as every fire-safety draft is marked.
- The workplace dialog grows a building group, optional, below the fire-safety group of ADR 018; a specialist who skips it loses nothing but rows of the sheet. Note of 2026-10-12: the building was built as a section "Clădirea" with a dialog of its own in each fixed space's card on the _Mijloace PSI_ tab, beside the installations it leaves to the inventory, not inside the workplace dialog.
- The documents table gains a period, the uniqueness of a type per client holds per period, and the listing, the generation request and the tab learn about years; the first set is untouched, since none of its types is dated.
- The provider still has to confirm what the packs cannot: see the questions below. Until then the gate follows the norm, not the provider's habit of issuing nine decisions to everyone.

## Order of work

1. This ADR, the amendments to ADR 012, 016, 018 and 019, the glossary in `CONTEXT.md` and `docs/data-model.md`.
2. The gate and the reorder: the ordinals, the manifest numbers and file names, the not-applicable types in the readiness and on the tab, the count on the generation form, cover 1.0 listing what exists, and the tab's order; the three gated types.
3. The schedules of the ADR 018 amendment, gated: the free rows per workplace, the merge data, the template 5.4.
4. The building block on the workplace, with its migration, contracts, API, dialog group and tests; the site sheet's merge data and template 7.1 with its gaps, and the section 7 of the tab.
5. The period on a document, the dated listing and generation, and the annual report's merge data and template 7.2.
6. After each merge, the templates are registered on hosted.

## Open with the provider

1. Whether she issues decisions 8 and 9 and the installation register to a client under ten, as the three packs suggest, or drops them as art. 20 allows.
2. How a temporary or mobile construction site is documented: what its posted sheet says, which extinguishers she counts and by what norm.
3. Whether she has ever filed a _fişa obiectivului_ with an inspectorate, in what form, and to which inspectorate when the points of work span counties.
4. For the substances list, whether she fills the mentions of Legea 307/2006 art. 19 lit. f) herself from the safety data sheets or asks the client for them.
5. For the annual report, whether she writes one per client today, and from what.
