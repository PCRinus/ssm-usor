# ADR 021: Training records per employee, and the two individual training sheets generated from them

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-10-16

## Context

Every document the app generates is a document of a client: one type, once per client, with its revisions ([ADR 005](adr-005-document-generation.md)), or once per period for a dated type ([ADR 020](adr-020-fire-safety-from-ten-employees.md)). Nothing is generated about one employee. The two documents an inspector asks for first after the decisions are both about one employee: the occupational safety **individual training sheet** (_fișa individuală de instruire în domeniul securității și sănătății în muncă_, HG 1425/2006 art. 81 and annex 11) and the fire-safety one (_fișa individuală de instructaj în domeniul situațiilor de urgență_, OMAI 712/2005 art. 45 to 50 and its annex, required of every operator by OMAI 163/2007 art. 18 lit. o) and art. 20 (3) lit. f)). The SSM contact named them on 2026-10-11; #183 asked for an ADR on training records and due dates, #425 for the fire-safety sheet, and [ADR 019](adr-019-fire-safety-stage-three.md) and ADR 020 left the sheets out until both could be designed together.

The two forms have one shape. An identification block: the name, the employee number, the home address, the date and place of birth, the blood group (SSM), the education (PSI), the qualification, the contract title, the workplace, the authorizations such as ISCIR (SSM) and the route and duration of the commute (SSM). Then the two hiring trainings, each with a date, a duration in hours, who trained and their function, what was taught, and the signatures; then "admis la lucru", the name, function, date and signature of the person who admitted the employee to work; then a table of periodic trainings, one row each: date, duration, occupation, material taught, signatures. The SSM form adds a table of supplementary periodic trainings, a block of test results, and blocks for accidents, sanctions, medical and psychological checks that other records fill. HG 1425/2006 art. 81 has the sheet name the material taught, its duration and its date, and since HG 259/2022 allows it in electronic form; OMAI 712/2005 art. 47 still reads "cu pastă sau cerneală". Mircea confirmed on 2026-10-16 that both sheets may be kept electronically; the ADR does not depend on it, since the signatures stay on paper until the electronic signature of the product scope exists.

Photographs of a filled pair of sheets from June 2026 were read on 2026-10-16, with the consent of the person. They show the practice behind the forms: the qualification, the education and the authorizations left blank; "locul de muncă" filled with the position's work zone, "Atelier, beneficiari"; the commute line filled with "cf. declarației", a separate declaration the employee signs; the SSM hiring trainings four hours each, the fire-safety ones eight, the periodic ones two; the periodic rows dated by month and year only, with the occupation and dealt article ranges of the IPSSM or the IPSU as the material, one range of the common part and one of the position's chapters, which is what the training themes of [ADR 014](adr-014-training-themes.md) and ADR 019 compute per session; and the signatures: on the SSM sheet three on every training, the trainee's, the trainer's and the verifier's, where the specialist signs as trainer and verifier of the introductory training and as verifier of the workplace one given by the workplace manager; on the fire-safety sheet two, the trainee's and the verifier's on the hiring trainings, the fire-safety technician's then the workplace manager's, and the trainee's and the trainer's on the periodic rows. The contact's note of the same day says the same for the hiring trainings and nothing of the periodic rows or of "admis la lucru".

What the app holds already covers most of the forms. The employee row carries the names, the number, the address, the birth date and place, the blood group and the contract title, and keeps training out on purpose: the employees migration says records belong in their own table, so that a flag is never wrong the day after a training expires. The job position gives the occupation, the staff category and so the interval, and its work zone. The responsible persons give the workplace managers, the organization the specialist and the fire-safety technician, the client its legal representative. The training themes 4.2 fix the two hiring plans at 240 minutes each and the fire-safety themes 3.1 at 480 minutes, both as static text of the templates; the periodic durations are the two schedules'. The themes' snapshot records every position's sessions with their month and content line, which ADR 014 kept "so the training sheets to come can copy a session's content line as the material taught". The trainer per staff category is `trainerOf` and the fire-safety trainers of ADR 019.

What the app holds nowhere: that a training happened, on what day, for whom, taught by whom, verified by whom, and that someone admitted the person to work; the qualification, the education, the authorizations and the commute; and at which of the client's workplaces an employee works, which #296 and #257 also wait for. The commute matters beyond the form: Legea 319/2006 art. 30 (1) lit. g) makes an accident on the normal route and within the normal duration of the commute a work accident, and the declared route is what an investigation measures against.

The competitor ssm.ro generates one sheet per training per employee from an approved theme, each signed on its own, and prints the sheet as a report of them. The provider's three packs hold no sheet of their own; the forms are bought printed and filled by hand.

The employees decision of 2026-09-17 ruled out a global list of employees across clients in favour of an exceptions view. On 2026-10-16 Mircea asked for a root page of all employees of the organization, searchable, since the lists grow past what sorting by name can serve; the exceptions view is what that page shows once training is recorded.

## Decision

### A training held is recorded once, and gives every attendee a training record

A **training held** (_instruire efectuată_) is one act of training at a client: its **domain**, occupational safety or fire safety; its **phase**, introductory general, workplace, periodic or supplementary; the day it was held; its duration in minutes; the material taught, as text; who trained, as a name and a function; who verified, as a name and a function, where the form has that signature; and for a supplementary training its reason, one of the cases of HG 1425/2006 art. 98: an absence of more than thirty working days, a change of the rules of the workplace or the own instructions, a return after an accident, special works, new equipment, a changed technology or procedure, a new one. It is recorded once, in one flow, with the employees who attended, and every attendee gets a **training record** (_instruire_, on the employee's page): the attendance of one employee at one training held, carrying the occupation printed for it, the name of the position at the time, since a person who changes post keeps the rows of the old one. A hiring training is a training held with one attendee; recording a single employee's training is the same flow with one person ticked. The sheet prints records; it never prints a training held that nobody attended.

The material and the duration are proposed from the plan and stored on the training held. A periodic training proposes the content line and the duration of the session of that month from the client's newest training themes revision, per position for occupational safety, per staff category for fire safety, and the record remembers the themes revision it was proposed from; a hiring training proposes the themes' static plan, 240 or 480 minutes, and its content line as the 4.2 or 3.1 template prints it; a supplementary training proposes nothing but the duration floor of one hour (art. 80^1). The specialist may change the text and the duration before saving: a session cut short or a material taught in another order is a fact the sheet must print as it was, and a client without generated themes has no proposal to take. Once saved, a record keeps its duration and material whatever the themes become: it says what happened, and the sheet prints it as it was. The proposal, not the record, follows the themes: a client whose themes come to carry plans of their own gets them proposed from then on, which today means the 4.2 and 3.1 templates' plans, since the hiring plans are static text and a plan edited in the Word file cannot be read back. Reading the material back from the Word file of the themes was rejected, as ADR 014 did; the snapshot holds the sessions for this.

The workplace training record of each domain also carries "admis la lucru": the day, the name and the function of the person who admitted the employee to work, proposed as the training's trainer, since the forms print the block under that training and the sample has the same workplace manager in both. Storing admission on the employee was rejected: there are two admissions, one per domain, with different dates in the sample.

Absences are not modelled by this ADR; the thirty-day case of art. 98 is recorded as the reason of the supplementary training that follows it, not derived from a leave record.

### What the employee gains

The employee row gains the facts the forms print and the app does not hold: the **qualification** (_calificarea_), the **education** (_studiile_), the **authorizations** (_autorizații_, "ISCIR" and the like), all optional free text, blank on the sheet when empty, as the sample leaves them; the **commute** (_traseul de deplasare_), the route as free text and its duration in minutes, optional on the form and required by the occupational safety sheet; and a **workplace**, the `client_workplaces` row the employee works at, optional on the form, filled by default when the client has one active workplace, and required by both sheets when it has more than one. "Locul de muncă" on the sheets prints the workplace's name and the position's work zone together, "Punct de lucru Str. Fabricii 3 – Atelier", or whichever of the two the client has, since the work zone alone does not say which site at a client with several, and a workplace alone does not say the kind of place.

A separate route declaration was rejected: the employee signs the sheet at the hiring trainings, on the page that prints the route, and the app records when the route changed; the printed-form habit of "cf. declarației" exists because the identification block has no signature of its own. Making the route required on the employee form was rejected too: a specialist entering a new hire rarely knows it, and an employee must be creatable before the sheet is printed. The sheet is where the law needs the route, so the sheet is what asks for it.

### The two sheets are generated on demand, as PDF, outside the documentation sets

An **individual training sheet** (_fișa individuală de instruire_ for occupational safety, _fișa individuală de instructaj în domeniul situațiilor de urgență_ for fire safety) is a **sheet**, not a document of a set: it has no row in `client_documents`, no revisions, no draft, no editor, no "Date modificate", no place on the two documents tabs and no entry in their readiness. Opening it builds it from the employee and the records at that moment, merges the built-in template with docxtemplater and converts it through the PDF Worker in one request, and streams the PDF; nothing is stored. Two templates are added to the built-in set, in the form of annex 11 and of the OMAI 712/2005 annex with the house layout, registered in versions like the others so that a legal change in the forms is tracked ([ADR 017](adr-017-legislation-monitoring.md)), and the manifest marks them per employee, so the tabs and the covers leave them out.

Using the documents machinery was rejected. A revision is a version of a document that was replaced; a sheet does not get replaced, it grows by a row after every training, and fifty employees times every session is a list no tab can carry. A Word draft was rejected with it: the form is bought printed and filled by hand, nothing in it is written by the specialist, and what gets signed must not be editable. Storing the generated PDF was rejected for now: until something is signed, the PDF of today is reproducible from the records, and a stored copy would only go stale.

What a sheet prints: the identification block from the employee, the position and the workplace; the hiring trainings from their records, each with its date, duration in hours, trainer and function, material, and the "admis la lucru" block from the workplace record, blank where there is no record yet, so that a sheet printed on the day of hiring is the form the paper flow needs; the periodic table from the periodic and, on the SSM sheet, the supplementary records, each row with the day, the duration in hours, the occupation and the material; the test-results block, the accidents, the sanctions and the medical blocks of the SSM form empty. Under the recorded rows the periodic table prints blank rows to the end of its page, for as long as the signatures are collected on paper: the printed sheet is the signed original and the pen needs somewhere to write. A constant turns the blank rows off when electronic signing ships. Pre-printing the planned sessions of the year as unsigned rows was rejected: a row on the sheet means a training that happened.

The sheet blocks on two facts only, through the same gap rules as the sets, returned by its own readiness and shown on the employee's page: the commute route on the SSM sheet, and the workplace on both when the client has more than one. Every other empty line prints empty, as the paper form does.

A leaver's sheets are archived, not discarded, as the provider discards the paper ones: the records, the files and the two sheets of a terminated employee stay as they are, printable from _Foști angajați_ for as long as the client exists, and nothing of them is deleted when the employee leaves or the client is archived. No retention period is set; none is prescribed for the sheet itself.

The employee's page gains a section _Instruire_ with two views, SSM and PSI, each listing that domain's records, the next due date and the sheet's download; the client's employees section gains a view _Instruiri_ listing the trainings held, with the flow that records one.

(Note of 2026-10-17: the view became a tab of the client's page of its own, _Instruiri ținute_, after _Instruire și responsabili_, each row naming its participants with a link to their training tab of its domain, because a second row of tabs under _Angajați_ was rejected; _Angajați_ is a plain list again.)

### Who signs what, prepared for the electronic signature

Each training record has fixed **signer slots** by role, and the roles map onto people the app holds: the trainee is the employee; on the occupational safety sheet the trainer and the verifier are the specialist for the introductory training, the workplace manager and the specialist for the workplace training, the trainer the themes name for the position and the specialist for a periodic or supplementary training; on the fire-safety sheet the one signature beside the trainee's is the fire-safety technician's on the introductory training, the workplace manager's on the workplace training, and the trainer's, the manager or the technician as the fire-safety themes name them, on a periodic row; "admis la lucru" is the workplace manager's, or the legal representative's where the client has none. The flow proposes these names and functions and the specialist may replace them, since the person who actually trained is the fact.

The training record is therefore the unit that will be signed, not the sheet: it is one person, one day, one material, one duration, three names, and it never changes once signed; the sheet is a view that reproduces signed rows. A `training_signatures` table, one row per record and role with the method, the time and the evidence, comes with the electronic signature and is not created now. Until then the paper sheet carries the signatures and the app records nothing about them; a record is editable and deletable by the organization's members, as any fact entered by hand.

The contact's rule has the specialist verify the workplace training, where HG 1425/2006 art. 94 names the trainer's hierarchical superior. The rule follows the contact and the sample as the default, and a record whose verifier was someone else says so in its slot.

### The due date is computed, never stored

For each employee and domain the app computes the **next training due** (_termenul următoarei instruiri_): the day of the last periodic record, else of the workplace training record, else the hire date, plus the interval: for occupational safety the position's own interval where it has one, else the client's interval for the position's staff category; for fire safety the fire-safety training schedule's interval for the category, which a position cannot override (#371). A supplementary training never moves it, as art. 98 gives it "suplimentar celei programate". HG 1425/2006 art. 96 sets the interval by the client's training programme, at most six months, twelve for technical-administrative staff, and says nothing of when a new hire's first interval starts; the workplace training is the day the person was admitted to work, and the hire date stands in until it is recorded. (Note of 2026-10-17: the interval and its source, the position or the client, are answered to the app with the due date, so the employee's page can say what the due date counts and where to change it. An untrained employee has no due date: before the first hiring training is recorded, the hire date plus the interval named a day long past for anyone hired years ago. The hire date still stands in after an introductory general training alone.)

From the due date, a **training state** per domain: _neinstruit_ while the employee has no hiring training recorded in that domain, _la zi_, _scadentă_ when the due date falls within the next thirty days, _depășită_ once it has passed. A terminated employee has none. The state is returned with the employee lists and on the page, and is what the Employees page filters by. Storing a `last_trained_at` on the employee was rejected, as the employees migration asked.

### History kept on paper comes in as records and as files

A client onboarded with sheets filled by hand gets its history two ways. A training held can be recorded for a past day with the source _de pe fișa de hârtie_, the material and the duration typed, the signatures understood to be on the paper; the minimum for the due dates is the hiring trainings and the last periodic training per employee. And a **client file** ([ADR 013](adr-013-client-files.md)) can be attached to an employee: the photograph or the scan of the paper sheet, uploaded from the employee's page and listed there, and under the client's files with the employee's name. The workers' representative's course certificate of [ADR 010](adr-010-conditional-documents.md) is such a file too. Extracting the rows of a photographed sheet into proposed records is a feature of its own, after the records exist. (Note of 2026-10-17: an employee's files are listed on the employee's page only. The client's "Alte documente" lists the client's own files, those of no employee, because the files of every employee there buried the client's own.)

### A page of the organization's employees, with search

The app gains a root page _Angajați_, every current employee of every client of the organization in one list, with the client, the position, the hire date and the two training states, filtered by client and by training state, sorted and paged as the other lists are, and searched by text: the name, the employee number and the client's name, matched without regard to diacritics or case, through the `unaccent` extension and an expression index, which the data-table plan reserved for this. The same search box goes on the client's employees list. The page is the exceptions view the 2026-09-17 decision asked for, with the whole list behind it; the dashboard keeps its counts.

## What is left out

- The electronic signature itself and the `training_signatures` table; the records are shaped for it.
- Tests and their results: the test block of the SSM sheet stays empty until #449 decides how tests are served.
- Membership of the first-intervention team per employee and its monthly sessions (ADR 019): the fire-safety sheet prints the same periodic rows for everyone until the themes get the team's block.
- Absences as dated events, and the supplementary training derived from them.
- Extracting records from photographs of paper sheets.
- The protective-equipment handover sheet and the risk-factor sheet of HG 355/2007 (#278), per employee too, on the same on-demand mechanism when asked for.
- The workplace manager per workplace (#296) and the risk assessment per point of work (#257): the employee's workplace is the link they need, and they remain their own work.
- Storing a sheet's PDF, or a "printed on" record.
- Spreadsheet import of employees, where the new fields will be columns.

## Consequences

- The app records events for the first time: a training held is a fact with a day and people, not a setting, and the employees migration's promise is kept: the employee row still carries no training flag.
- A second kind of generated output exists beside the documents: a sheet, per employee, built on demand and never stored. The engine's template registry learns a per-employee flag, the PDF Worker is called outside issuing, and local development needs the converter running to open a sheet.
- The employee form grows five optional fields and a workplace; the sheets, not the form, refuse to print without the route and, at a multi-site client, the workplace.
- "Locul de muncă" has a fourth meaning in the source forms, the place a person works, and the glossary says what the sheets print for it.
- The glossary's training session stays the planned session of the themes; a training held is what happened, and a training record is one person's attendance.
- Client files can belong to an employee; the client's files list shows whose. (Note of 2026-10-17: it no longer does; it holds the client's own files only, and an employee's stay on their page.)
- The employees API gains an organization-wide list with text search; the first index on a function of a text column, and the first use of `unaccent`.
- The old decision against a global employee list is reversed in part: the list exists, with the training states that make it an exceptions view.

## Defaults taken instead of asking the provider

Settled with Mircea on 2026-10-16 as defaults a record can depart from, since each is one slot or one proposal and none changes the shape of the data:

1. The specialist verifies the workplace training, as the contact's note and the sample say; a client where the trainer's superior signs (HG 1425/2006 art. 94) names them in the verifier slot of that record.
2. No planned session is ever pre-printed on a sheet; a row is a training that happened.
3. The hiring durations are proposed from the client's themes as generated, 240 and 480 minutes today, and a record keeps what was saved; a per-client plan becomes a proposal only once the themes hold it as data.
4. A fire-safety periodic row has one trainer: the workplace manager is proposed, and the fire-safety technician replaces them in the slot when the technician trained, since the form has one column for the signature.
5. A leaver's sheets are kept, not discarded: see the sheets' section.
